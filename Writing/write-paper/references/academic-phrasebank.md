# Academic Phrasebank (Bilingual Calibration Reference)

Use these alternatives when drafting, calibrating claim strength, or rewriting Chinese manuscripts into idiomatic English. Choose the plainest phrase that fits the evidence; do not stack formal connectives into every sentence.

## 1. Verbs by Evidence Strength

Matching the main verb to the strength of the study design and data is the primary tool for claim calibration.

### Strong (solid experimental design, statistical support, and direct observation)

- **English**: `show`, `demonstrate`, `establish`, `reveal`, `identify`, `confirm`
- **Chinese**: `表明`、`证实`、`确立`、`揭示`

### Medium (consistent empirical pattern, reasonable but bounded or non-causal)

- **English**: `suggest`, `indicate`, `support the view that`, `be consistent with`, `point to`
- **Chinese**: `提示`、`说明`、`支持……的看法`、`与……一致`、`指向`

### Weak (mechanistic speculation, extrapolation beyond direct measurements)

- **English**: `may reflect`, `could arise from`, `appears to`, `seems likely`, `might be explained by`
- **Chinese**: `可能反映`、`或源于`、`似乎`、`看来`、`或可解释为`

### Describing the evidence base itself

- **Weak / sparse**: `limited`, `scant`, `insufficient`, `preliminary`
- **Accumulating**: `growing`, `emerging`, `accumulating`
- **Strong**: `consistent`, `reliable`, `convincing`, `substantial`

## 2. Describing the Research Gap (Exact, Not Theatrical)

State what remains unknown precisely rather than dramatizing prior literature as blind.

- **Recommended (English)**: `remains poorly understood`, `has not been examined under...`, `has received limited attention`, `few studies have addressed...`, `empirical evidence remains sparse for...`
- **Recommended (Chinese)**: `仍不甚清楚`、`尚未在……情形下被检验`、`受到的关注有限`、`鲜有研究处理……`、`关于……的证据仍很稀少`
- **Avoid**: `no one has ever studied`, `completely unknown`, `ignored by all previous work` (rarely survives peer review).

## 3. Comparing with Prior Work Fairly

### Expressing agreement

- **English**: `These results are consistent with...`, `This finding accords with...`, `Our observations broadly support...`
- **Chinese**: `这些结果与……一致`、`这一发现与……相符`、`我们的观察大体支持……`

### Marking differences without belittling

- **English**: `In contrast to earlier reports...`, `This finding differs from...`, `One plausible reason for this discrepancy is...`
- **Chinese**: `与早先的报道不同……`、`这一发现不同于……`、`造成这一差异的一个可能原因是……`

### Framing baselines without building a straw man

- **English**: `Although previous studies showed X, their performance under Y remains unclear.` / `Earlier work established X, but did not address Y.`
- **Chinese**: `尽管已有研究展示了……，但它们在……方面的表现仍不清楚。` / `早先的工作确立了……，但没有处理……。`

## 4. Stating Real Limitations

Tie every limitation to a concrete boundary in data, assumptions, or compute; never write empty ritual apologies.

- **English**: `These findings should be interpreted with caution because...`, `A limitation of this study is that...`, `The generalizability of these results is bounded by...`, `We cannot exclude the possibility that...`
- **Chinese**: `这些发现应谨慎解读，因为……`、`本研究的一个局限是……`、`这些结果的可推广性受限于……`、`我们无法排除……的可能。`

## 5. Implications and Future Work

Keep implications inside the scope of the results, and tie future work to specific open boundaries.

- **Implications (English)**: `An implication of this finding is that...`, `These results may help explain...`, `This work has practical implications for...`
- **Implications (Chinese)**: `由此引出的一个含义是……`、`这些发现或有助于解释……`、`这项工作对……具有参考价值。`
- **Future work (English)**: `Further work is needed to determine whether...`, `Future studies should examine...`, `A natural next step is to test...`
- **Future work (Chinese)**: `还需进一步工作来确定是否……`、`未来研究应考察……`、`下一步可进一步检验……`

## 6. Minimal Transitions and Paragraph Openings

Use the lightest connector that clarifies logic:

- **Contrast**: `however`, `by contrast`, `nevertheless`, `despite this`, `whereas`
- **Addition**: `furthermore`, `in addition`, `moreover`, `also`
- **Cause / effect**: `therefore`, `thus`, `consequently`, `as a result`, `thereby`
- **Qualification**: `in part`, `approximately`, `at least in this cohort`, `under this setting`

**Avoid repetitive `This + verb` openings**: Do not start consecutive sentences or paragraphs with `This suggests...` or `This shows...`. Name the referent explicitly (`Such heterogeneity...`, `The resulting latency drop...`), use a compact participial bridge (`Taken together, ...`), or let adjacent sentences connect through clear subject continuity without a transition word.

## 7. Common Chinglish-to-Idiomatic Academic Repairs

- **Redundant category verbs**: `carry out an investigation into` -> `investigate`; `make a comparison between` -> `compare`; `realize the optimization of` -> `optimize`.
- **Wordy connectors**: `in order to` -> `to` (unless emphasizing purpose after a negative); `due to the fact that` -> `because`; `at the present time` -> `currently`; `a total of 50 samples` -> `50 samples`.
- **Topic-prominent clauses**: `For existing methods, they suffer from high latency.` -> `Existing methods incur high latency.`
